3. Privacy and targeting boundary

Advertisers and their providers must not target, profile, infer, purchase, or receive data about a person from their trades, P&L, ROI, risk, leverage, broker connection, account balance, psychology, notes, screenshots, private group activity, or journal behavior.

  • Contextual advertising by public page topic is the default.
  • Personalized advertising requires a separate, informed consent flow where required by law.
  • Consent signals must be respected, recorded, and withdrawn without dark patterns.
  • Ad identifiers must be kept separate from JournalEdge account and journal identifiers.
  • Third-party ad scripts must not execute in private or authenticated areas.
  • Advertisers may not attempt to re-identify JournalEdge users or combine placement data with private trading data.

Advertisers receive only the campaign information and aggregate reporting expressly agreed for the placement. Revenue, impressions, clicks, and consent rates are reported in aggregate whenever possible.

4. Creative, landing pages, and prohibited content

Advertisers are responsible for the accuracy, legality, accessibility, security, and availability of their creative and destination pages. All claims must be substantiated and suitable for the audience and jurisdiction where shown.

Prohibited or restricted content

  • Guaranteed profits, risk-free returns, “sure win” signals, or claims that imply certainty of investment performance.
  • Unlicensed financial services, misleading broker promotions, or offers that violate securities, commodities, consumer-protection, gambling, or advertising laws.
  • Malware, phishing, credential collection, deceptive downloads, forced redirects, or unsafe landing pages.
  • Discriminatory, hateful, abusive, sexually explicit, illegal, or exploitative content.
  • Fake urgency, fake endorsements, fabricated reviews, impersonation, or misleading JournalEdge branding.
  • Creative that imitates a trading signal, JournalEdge alert, account warning, or system notification.

We may reject, request changes to, pause, or remove creative that violates these terms, a platform policy, a regulator requirement, or our user-safety standards.

5. Sponsorships, affiliates, and disclosures

Sponsored content must be clearly labeled with language such as “Sponsored,” “Advertisement,” or “Affiliate link” near the promotional content. Disclosure must be understandable on mobile, visible before a reasonable user acts, and not hidden behind a hover state, collapsed control, or ambiguous icon.

JournalEdge may earn a commission from an affiliate link. A link or sponsorship is not an endorsement, guarantee, recommendation, or verification of the advertiser, product, broker, exchange, educator, or financial outcome. Users should perform their own due diligence.

Sponsored educational content must remain educational, must not masquerade as independent editorial content, and must not contain personalized financial advice. JournalEdge may require a risk disclosure appropriate to the content and jurisdiction.

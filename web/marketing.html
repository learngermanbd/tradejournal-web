Clear answers

Good tools should explain themselves.

Here is the short version of what JournalEdge stores, calculates, and protects.

Where is my trading data stored?

Account settings and control metadata use Supabase. Private journal data and attachments are encrypted before Cloudflare R2 storage. The browser is not the permanent data store.

Can I use JournalEdge for options and crypto?

Yes. JournalEdge supports stocks, ETFs, forex, crypto, futures, options, indices, commodities, bonds, CFDs, and custom instruments.

Will ads use my trades or psychology?

No. JournalEdge does not load advertising scripts in the private app, journal, psychology, balances, screenshots, or admin areas.

Can I export or delete my data?

Yes. Export, deletion, recovery, consent, and sharing-revoke controls are part of the product boundary and are recorded in the appropriate audit history.

1. Scope and controller

This Privacy Policy applies to JournalEdge, including the public marketing website at learngermanwith.fun, the user application at app.learngermanwith.fun, related support communications, and future public advertising placements controlled by JournalEdge. The admin application is protected and follows the same privacy and security boundaries.

JournalEdge is a record-keeping, analytics, and education tool. It is not a broker, investment adviser, exchange, custodian, or provider of personalized financial advice.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Account and identity information

When you create or access an account, we may receive your email address, display name, profile settings, authentication provider identifier, language, timezone, and session/security metadata. If you use Google, Apple, Microsoft, GitHub, Telegram, or another provider, that provider sends only the information permitted by your authorization and its own privacy policy.

2.2 Trading journal information

You may choose to enter symbols, market, direction, quantity, entry and exit prices, stop loss, take profit, leverage, fees, P&L, ROI, strategy, notes, psychology, screenshots, attachments, account labels, and review records. This may reveal sensitive financial behavior. We treat it as private journal content and do not use it for advertising targeting.

2.3 Technical and usage information

We may collect IP address, browser and device type, approximate region, operating system, request timestamps, diagnostic events, security logs, page interaction events, and aggregate performance data needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the service. We minimize this data and do not use private journal text as general analytics content.

2.4 Support and transactions

If you contact support, we receive the information you include. If paid plans are enabled, payment processors handle payment card details; JournalEdge receives limited billing metadata such as plan, transaction status, invoice identifiers, and a payment-provider customer identifier. We do not intend to store full payment card numbers.

3. How we use information

  • Provide authentication, account access, cloud synchronization, journal storage, calculations, imports, reports, and settings.
  • Calculate P&L, risk, reward, R-multiple, ROI, drawdown, and other requested analytics.
  • Protect accounts, detect abuse, enforce permissions, rate-limit requests, and investigate security incidents.
  • Respond to support requests, send service messages, and provide transactional notices.
  • Process subscriptions, entitlements, refunds, invoices, and payment-support events where paid features are enabled.
  • Understand aggregate product performance and improve reliability without exposing private journal content to advertising systems.
  • Comply with legal obligations, enforce agreements, and protect the rights and safety of users and the service.

We do not sell private trading journals, notes, psychology, screenshots, balances, broker data, or P&L data.

JournalEdge uses a cloud-first architecture. The browser is not the permanent source of truth for saved data.

5. When information may be shared

We may share limited information with service providers that process data on our instructions, such as hosting, authentication, storage, email, payment, monitoring, or optional integration providers. Providers must receive only the data needed for their function and are expected to maintain appropriate safeguards.

We may disclose information when required by law, valid legal process, fraud prevention, emergency protection, corporate restructuring, or to enforce our terms. We do not provide advertisers with private journal records.

If you intentionally use a future sharing or coach feature, the information you select may be shown to the recipients and scope you choose. You can revoke a share where the feature provides a revoke control, but recipients may retain lawful copies made before revocation.

6. Advertising, cookies, and analytics

Public advertising, if enabled, is designed to be contextual and limited to intentionally public surfaces. Ads must not be targeted from trades, P&L, psychology, balances, screenshots, broker data, or private account activity. The private user application, journal, psychology screens, balances, screenshots, and admin panel must not load third-party advertising scripts.

We may use strictly necessary cookies or similar technologies for authentication, security, preferences, and basic operations. Optional analytics, personalization, or advertising technologies should be activated only where required consent has been collected. You may withdraw optional consent through the available consent controls; withdrawal does not affect processing that is strictly necessary to provide the service.

See the separate Advertising Terms & Conditions for advertiser and sponsored-content rules.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, delete, restrict, object to, or request portability of personal information. You may also withdraw consent where processing relies on consent and complain to a data-protection authority.

  • Update profile and settings through the application.
  • Request an export of account and journal data by contacting support.
  • Request account or journal deletion, subject to lawful retention, fraud prevention, billing, or backup limitations.
  • Opt out of optional advertising personalization and non-essential analytics.
  • Disconnect optional integrations and request deletion of provider connection metadata.

To protect accounts, we may verify identity before completing a request. We aim to respond within the period required by applicable law.

8. Retention and deletion

We retain account and journal information while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. We retain security, billing, audit, and legal records for the period needed for legitimate business, compliance, dispute resolution, and fraud-prevention purposes. Deleted data may remain temporarily in encrypted backups until the backup cycle expires, after which it is removed or rendered inaccessible according to our procedures.

9. Children and international transfers

JournalEdge is not directed to children who cannot lawfully consent to data processing. Do not use the service if you are prohibited from doing so by the law that applies to you.

Our providers may process information in countries different from your own. Where required, we use appropriate contractual, organizational, or legal safeguards for international transfers.

10. Policy changes

We may update this policy when the product, providers, laws, or data practices change. We will update the date above and, where appropriate, provide an in-product or email notice for material changes.

11. Contact

Questions, access requests, deletion requests, and privacy complaints can be sent to hello@learngermanwith.fun.

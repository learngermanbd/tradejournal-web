Application blueprint

A free-first trading workspace with a secure control plane.

The Android client keeps journal data local by default. The backend manages identity, roles, subscriptions, integrations, and aggregate service health without exposing private trade notes. The blueprint prioritizes free developer tiers without treating them as production guarantees.

What each layer owns

Keeping responsibilities narrow makes the app easier to secure, test, and scale.

01

Android client

Forms, navigation, local calculations, offline journaling, accessibility, notifications, and user-controlled sync.

02

Private data layer

Trades, notes, emotions, screenshots, accounts, and reports. Stored locally and encrypted before any backup.

03

Backend server

Identity, role enforcement, billing, broker secrets, cloud AI consent, webhooks, and operational metadata.

04

Admin area

Aggregate-only monitoring. Every privileged action is authenticated, authorized, and audit logged.

Important boundary

The admin app is not the user app.

They may share a design system, but production authorization must come from the backend.

  • User role reads and writes only their own permitted resources.
  • Admin role reads aggregate metadata and operational records.
  • Broker secrets live behind a restricted service boundary.
  • Trade content stays local or in the user-controlled encrypted vault.
  • Every admin mutation gets an audit event.
